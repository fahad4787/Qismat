Product overview

Qismat: free prize draws, paid for by ads

Players in Pakistan watch short ads to earn tickets and spend them to enter prize draws. Nobody pays to play. The ad revenue pays for the prizes, and you run everything from the admin panel.

How it works

  1. Sign upGoogle or email, plus a mobile number
  2. Watch an ad+1 ticket when it plays to the end
  3. Enter a drawSpend tickets on the prize you want
  4. Draw closesAdmin picks winners live
  5. Prize deliveredWinner is called and published

The rules

20tickets a day from ads, at most
+2tickets for the daily claim, which builds a streak
Rs 0cost to play. Tickets cannot be bought

Android app

For players

Sign in

A short intro, then sign in with Google or email and password. A mobile number is required so winners can be reached.

Home

Ticket balance, today's progress (for example 5 of 20), the daily claim and featured draws.

Watch ad

A rewarded ad. The ticket counts only if the ad finishes. Closing it early, having no connection or having no ad available shows a clear message.

Draws

Live prizes with photo, value, tickets per entry, number of winners and closing time. Players can buy several entries at once, and more entries mean better odds.

Winners

Every result is published with first name, city, prize and date.

Profile

Tickets, streak, ads watched, the sound on/off switch and sign out. Invite a friend (5 tickets each) coming soon

Web · /admin

Admin panel

Access

One admin email and password. An account gets in only if it is listed as an admin in Firebase.

Overview

Revenue today and all time (ads × eCPM × rupee rate), ads watched, new players, open draws and the total prize pool. Shows charts of ads and new players per day.

Draws

Add, edit, end early or delete a draw. Set the title, prize value, tickets per entry, number of winners, closing time, tag and photo. Use "Next round" to reopen a finished prize.

Live draw

Pick a closed draw and spin. Each entry is one chance, and nobody wins twice. Winners are picked with secure randomness and each one's odds are shown.

Winners

Add or edit winners and track each payout: To contact → Contacted → Delivered.

Players

Search by name and see each player's mobile, tickets, ads, streak and entries. You can set a player's ticket balance for support cases.

Settings

eCPM and rupees per dollar (for the revenue figures), the website announcement banner and the Google Play link.
